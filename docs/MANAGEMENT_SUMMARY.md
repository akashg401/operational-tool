# Operations Toolkit - Management Summary

## Objective

Reduce manual effort in insurance operations through automation.

## Modules Delivered

### PDF Splitter

Manual effort reduced for policy segregation.

### PDF Merger

Eliminates manual PDF consolidation.

### CSV Formatter

Converts raw customer data into portal-ready upload files.

### Dolphin Upload Automation

Generates upload-compatible XLSX files without manual Excel intervention.

### Policy Downloader

Downloads issued policy PDFs in bulk.

### Hub-wise Downloader

Automatically segregates downloaded policies by hub.

### Policy Report Merger

Combines multiple reports into a single report while preserving hyperlinks.

## Benefits

* Reduced manual processing time
* Standardized output formats
* Lower operational errors
* Faster turnaround times
* Scalable workflow for multiple users

## Current Status

Production-ready for internal operational use.

* Unicode normalization
